This is the LaTeX source of *Erdős–Hajnal for graphs with no 5-hole* by Maria Chudnovsky, Alex Scott, Paul Seymour, and Sophie Spirkl, [arXiv:2102.04994v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.04994v1), matching the supplied paper.

Source: https://arxiv.org/src/2102.04994v1. The paper’s text, authorship, statements, proofs, and preamble are unchanged. Added `% lax begin` / `% lax end` comments connect passages to archive entries. Statement begin markers sit immediately inside their theorem environments, after the environment has ended the preceding paragraph. This avoids highlights starting on that paragraph’s last line without adding paragraph breaks or changing the printed spacing.

The annotations cover the five-cycle argument in Sections 2–4, its introductory statement, and the definitions used there. All eight existing concepts have at least one annotation. Six of the seven published proofs have corresponding passages: the paper cites Rödl’s theorem without proving it, whereas the formalization supplies a proof from regularity, so no paper passage is labeled as that additional proof. Later results about other excluded graphs are outside this submission’s scope.

The links identify the integral adaptations documented in the concept cards, rather than asserting literal equality of every displayed inequality. The bipartite comb result specializes Theorem 2.1 to `d = 1/2` with constant 128 and four-adic peeling. The critical-comb result uses reciprocal integer parameters and constant `2^20`. The averaging lemma retains the existing factor 4 degree bound. The critical-comb consequence packages parameter choices and comb extraction within the proof of Theorem 4.4. The final theorem uses an equivalent positive integer exponent.
